التطبيق يعمل كاملاً بدون حساب، وتبقى بياناتك على جهازك فقط.

Privacy Policy — Muhassaba

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Muhassaba is a personal Islamic app for daily self-accountability. We respect your privacy; this policy explains what we store and how we use it.

What the app stores

• App data: your deeds, adhkar, the sins you are fighting, your daily history and settings. Stored on your device. • If you create an account or sign in: your name and email (and phone number if you enter it) through Firebase Authentication, and your app data is synced to your account on Firebase Firestore so it returns on any device. • Firebase services may collect minimal technical data (such as an installation ID) to operate.

Using the app without an account

The app works fully without an account, and your data stays on your device only.

Reminders and notifications

The app sends local notifications from your own device (the daily message and your reminders). Reminder times and the texts you write are stored on your device only and are never sent to any server. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob ads. Google may use your device advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings (Google, then Ads).

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone, except the service providers that run the app (Google: Firebase and AdMob) under their own policies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any deed or sin inside the app at any time. To delete your account and the data saved in the cloud, contact us via: a.elgohary330@gmail.com

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will show the last-updated date at the top.

Contact

For any privacy question: a.elgohary330@gmail.com
